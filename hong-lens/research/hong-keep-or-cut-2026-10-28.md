## Per stock

### TOA — watch
- Story: growth came from margin recovery off a 2022 trough, not sales (revenue 2023→25 flat). Current pace +10% TTM, Q2 +9% y-y and −25% q-q.
- PEG passes only on the low-base 3-y CAGR (0.31); on current growth 0.90 → fails ≤ 0.67.
- Catalyst: none for growth. GM 40% is a record while oil/TiO2 costs rose (deep dive §1.3) — downside risk, not catalyst.
- What would make it a cut: Q3/26 y-y profit negative or GM back below ~37%. Until then it is a cheap, cash-rich stock that is not a Hong growth stock.

### SISB — cut (as a Hong growth stock)
- Story broken: earnings falling 3 quarters straight (H1 −10%), company cut its 2026 student target 4,800 → 4,600 and froze fees; brokers see 2026 −6–8%, 2027–28 +2–4% (deep dive §1.1).
- PEG: 0.25 on the 2022–25 CAGR is the past (two campus openings); forward growth negative → PEG not meaningful.
- Catalyst: Marina campus opens Aug 2027 and is a cost before profit (breakeven 400 students = 2027–28).
- Fails Hong criteria 3 (margin) / 6 (new high) / 7 (growth). What remains is a cheap dividend stock — a different leg.

### SECURE — cut (as a Hong growth stock), confirm on Q3
- Story broken for now: Q2/26 EPS −58%, H1 −22%, TTM 1.05 below FY2025 1.18 → not at a profit high. First-year software sales (the growth engine) −56% in Q2; recurring MA revenue still +21% (deep dive §1.2).
- PEG: 0.41 on 3-y CAGR; current growth negative → n/a.
- Catalyst: no backlog figure in any document; 70% of revenue tied to Palo Alto.
- Q4 is the biggest quarter (35% of 2025 revenue); for profit to stay at a 5-y high, H2/26 needs ≥ +22% — Q3 (mid-Nov) is the check. Rated cut rather than watch because the growth engine has already stopped in the latest data; Art may prefer to wait one quarter.

### MOSHI — go on
- Story intact: H1/26 EPS +22%, revenue +17% (inside 15–20% guidance), GM 56.5% up, ROE 29%, no bank debt.
- PEG 0.66 on 3-y EPS CAGR (just passes); 0.75 on H1 pace; forward PE 13.2 ≤ 15 passes.
- Catalyst: ~35 new stores/yr still running. Weak point: SSSG slowing (Q1 3.8% · Q2 4.0% · broker Q3 est. 1–2%, Sept 0% from floods per deep dive §1.2) → growth now depends on new stores.
- What would make it a cut: Q3/26 profit growth below ~10% y-y, or SSSG negative.
